# Rakamin | Kimia Farma


## **About Project**
The Project-Based Internship Program, a collaboration between Rakamin Academy and Kimia Farma Big Data Analytics, is designed to develop practical skills in Big Data Analytics. Participants complete learning modules, company coaching sessions, weekly assessments, and a final project that serves as a professional portfolio.

### **About Kimia Farma**
PT Kimia Farma (Persero) Tbk has grown into an integrated healthcare company in Indonesia. This growth is supported by its activities in pharmaceutical manufacturing, research and development, distribution and trading, marketing, pharmaceutical retail, clinical laboratories, and health clinics.
To ensure the company’s sustainability in line with Kimia Farma vision of becoming the premier integrated healthcare company that generates sustainable value, Kimia Farma continues to innovate and collaborate to create harmony across the entire business chain, from upstream to downstream.
